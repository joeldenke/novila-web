About Novila

What does Novila cost?

Nothing. Novila is free, with no subscription and no ads.

When is Novila coming to Google Play?

Soon. A small group is testing it right now. To join them, sign up as a tester and you'll get an invitation through Google Play.

Which phones does Novila work on?

Android 8.0 or later, on phones, tablets and foldables. The app is in English and Swedish and follows your phone's language.

Is Novila on the iPhone?

Not today. Novila is made for Android. If you'd like it on the iPhone, write to novila@joeldenke.se, so we know how many of you there are.

Why doesn't Novila use AI?

So that every suggestion can be explained. The plan, your readiness and the insights come from fixed rules on your phone, built on the WHO's and the Swedish Food Agency's advice, and the app always shows why it suggests something. A language model can sound sure without being right, and that doesn't belong anywhere near your body.

Does Novila replace healthcare?

No. Novila gives general advice on training, sleep and food and is not a medical device. Contact a healthcare provider about pain, injury or problems that don't go away, and call your emergency number if it's urgent.

Your data

Where is what I log stored?

Only on your phone, encrypted with a key from the phone's Android Keystore. Novila has no account and no server, and Android's backup leaves the app's data out.

What does it mean that the app has no internet access?

Novila doesn't have Android's internet permission, so the phone won't let it send anything, and neither can any library inside the app. The build stops if a new permission sneaks in. The links to the privacy policy and the terms open in your browser, not in the app.

How do I move to a new phone?

Choose Save a copy of my data in Settings and save the file somewhere your new phone can reach, such as Google Drive. On the new phone, choose Read in a saved copy.

The copy isn't encrypted, so keep it where only you can get to it. Reminders and the app's settings don't come along.

How do I delete everything?

Choose Delete all data in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes everything too. The developer has never received any of your data, so there is nothing more to delete.

What do you learn about me if I become a tester?

The form gives us your email address and your answers. The address is used only to invite you to the test and email you about it, and is deleted when the test is over. More in the privacy policy.

Watches

Do I need a watch?

No. You can log everything yourself. With a watch, steps, workouts, nights and recovery fill in by themselves.

Which watches work?

The ones that share their data with Health Connect, Android's shared place for health data. Most watches and their apps do. Check in your watch's app that Health Connect is turned on.

What does Novila read from Health Connect?

Only what you allow: today's steps and active calories, your workouts from the last five weeks, your nights, and resting heart rate and HRV from the last two weeks. Novila reads only while the app is open, writes nothing there and keeps nothing from it.

Training, sleep and food

Is Novila for me if I haven't trained in a long time?

Yes. The coach asks about your goal, your days and your time, starts gently and makes things harder only once you manage your sets. A walk counts as training.

I have sensitive knees or back pain. Can I train with Novila?

Gentle training has three levels focused on the knee, lower back, shoulder, neck or hip, and the coach can leave out jumps and lunges. It is general training, not care: if a physiotherapist has given you exercises, follow them.

How is the food score worked out?

You tap in servings per food group, and the score from 0 to 100 shows how the day compares with the Swedish Food Agency's dietary advice. Vegetables and fruit weigh the most, and one sweet thing a day is free.

Can I count calories in Novila?

No. Novila counts servings, not calories. You can log your weight and measurements and see a smooth trend line, but the app sets no weight goals.

How does Follow your cycle work?

It is off until you turn it on in My goals. You log period days and how your body feels, and on days with a symptom the coach offers a lighter session. You choose. Novila never predicts ovulation and is not contraception. Turning it off deletes everything you logged.
